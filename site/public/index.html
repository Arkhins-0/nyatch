A notch for Windows.

The time, your music and the weather at the top of the screen. Your apps, files and chats at the bottom.

Point at the notch. Scroll on it. Try the tab on the right, and the one at the bottom.

Ctrl Alt N

Closed, it is the time.

Point at it and it opens. The mouse wheel turns it through its pages: the song that is playing with its lyrics in time, the month, the day's weather hour by hour, how hard your PC is working, and a tray to keep files near.

It hides for films and games, unless you want its overlay.

Then a second one, at the bottom.

For the things you reach for all day. Ctrl Alt N opens it, then one letter opens a page: A your apps, F the file tray, V the clipboard, B notifications, T focus, N notes and to-dos, O online, R a terminal.

Focus has a timer, a stopwatch, and alarms that ring until you stop them. Space snoozes, Enter stops.

A chat at the edge of the screen.

Click the tab and the panel slides in; click anywhere else and it is gone. Ctrl Alt L does the same. Drag its edge to make it a quarter, a half, or all of the screen.

Use your own key for Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, Mistral, Grok, Groq, Perplexity or OpenRouter. Keys stay in Windows' Credential Manager and go only to the service you ask. Send a screenshot, a picture or a file, or talk to it.

Give it a face.

A theme brings a picture for every moment, a colour and a font. She looks up when the volume changes, sleeps through Do not disturb, and panics when an alarm goes off.

Bocchi the Rock! and Miku are in Settings › Theme now. Nyatch comes with an editor to make your own.
