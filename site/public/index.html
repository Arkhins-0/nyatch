Point at it. It opens.

Closed, it is the time. Open, the wheel turns it through its pages.

Player and lyrics
Whatever is playing, with lyrics in time, word by word where they exist.
Calendar and weather
The month with today marked, and the day ahead hour by hour.
Your PC
Processor, graphics, memory, disk, and how fast things come and go.
A tray for files
Drop a file on it to keep it near; drag it out where you need it.

A second notch for the rest.

At the bottom of the screen. Ctrl Alt N, then a letter, opens any of its pages.

Also on it: the clipboard, notifications with Do not disturb, notes and to-dos, and a terminal.

Ask, from the edge of the screen.

Click the tab and the panel slides in. Click anywhere else and it slides away.
