The top notch

Hover it, and it opens.

Closed, it shows the time, or what is playing. Open, it turns through its pages. Notices for the volume, brightness and the rest show under it, out of your way.

Clock

The time with seconds if you like, the date, and a line of your own above it.

Player and lyrics

Any app that plays: cover, controls and a live wave. Lyrics in time with the song, word by word where they can be.

Weather

Now and the next day, hour by hour, with today's high and low, wind, sunrise and sunset.

Calendar

The month at a glance, today marked.

Hardware

Processor, graphics, memory, disk, and your upload and download speeds.

File tray

Drop files on the notch to keep them close; drag them out where you need them.

The second notch

Your everyday tools, one hover away.

At the bottom of the screen, or wherever you place it. Each page has a letter: Ctrl + Alt + N, then the letter, opens it.

Apps

Pinned, open and recent apps, a click away.

Clipboard

What you copied, pictures too, with pins for what you need again.

Notifications

Everything Windows told you, in one list, with Do not disturb.

Chats

Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini, with your own keys, kept safe in Windows' Credential Manager.

Focus

A timer, a stopwatch and a pomodoro, with a sound when time is up.

Notes and to-dos

Lists and notes that stay on this PC.

Terminal

Command Prompt and PowerShell in tabs, right there.

Online

Ping, a speed test, and your sites with whether each one is up.

Characters

A little company, if you want it.

Character packs add pictures and short animations to the open pages: one for each time of day, the weather, a busy PC, an empty tray, a chat that is thinking.

They are off until you turn them on. Packs are downloaded from the Characters page in Nyatch's settings, kept up to date on their own, and removed with one click.

Made to fit in

Looks like it belongs on Windows.

  • Mica and acrylic backdrops, your accent colour or the album's.
  • A pill, a Windows 11 shape or a true notch shape.
  • Hides for full-screen apps, and stays out of games unless you want its game overlay.
  • Folder sizes in File Explorer, if you turn it on.
  • Settings in the style of Windows' own settings apps.

Free. Installs in a few seconds, and updates itself.
